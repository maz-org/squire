Scenario Phase
Scenarios are self-contained missions designed to be
played in a single sitting. The campaign of Gloomhaven
spans numerous scenarios, and most of your time
in the campaign will be spent playing through these
scenarios. The scenario book contains 101 scenarios, but
you will not play through them all, and they will not
be played in order. The story contains many branching
paths and detours.
SCENARIO BOOK
Each Scenario Phase consists of three distinct steps:
Beginning a Scenario: In this step, the party
will prepare everything needed for the scenario,
including their own characters, the monsters
they will fight against, and the map layout where
the fight will occur.
Playing a Scenario: This step occurs over a
series of rounds. Within each round, all figures
on the map, both characters and monsters, will

after round until the scenario is lost or completed
Ending a Scenario: In this step, the party
will resolve the effects of losing or completing
the scenario.
